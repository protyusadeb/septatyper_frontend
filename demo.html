Physicochemical syntax engine

Context-dependent grammar of ciprofloxacin resistance

Resistance does not follow from a single mutation. The effect of a residue depends on its neighbours: the same Glu at ParC-84 is susceptible beside a polar Ser80 and resistant beside a hydrophobic Ile80. SeptaTyper reads GyrA-83, GyrA-87, ParC-80 and ParC-84 as a combination, the tetratype, then adds RecN-346 and two qnr gene families to form the septatype.

The two-tier grammar

Tier 1

Tetratype baseline

Each position casts a vote toward resistance when it loses its native hydroxyl or negative charge. Two or more votes give a resistant baseline, none a susceptible one, and a single vote is settled by hydrophobicity.

Tier 2

Septatype overrides

qnr genes, selected RecN-346 states and species-specific patterns can overturn a susceptible baseline. They never turn a resistant baseline back into a susceptible one.

Mechanisms

Susceptible state

e.g. S-D-S-E

The polar hydroxyl (-OH) groups of GyrA-83 (Ser) and ParC-80 (Ser), combined with the negative charges of GyrA-87 (Asp) and ParC-84 (Glu), maintain the local electrostatic environment. This facilitates the water-metal ion bridge that coordinates the Mg²⁺ ion, allowing ciprofloxacin to bind successfully.

Hydrophobic disruption

e.g. I-N-I-E, L-G-I-E

Replacing Ser83 or Ser80 with hydrophobic residues (Ile, Leu, Phe) abolishes native hydrogen bonding. Replacing Asp87 with charge-neutral amino acids (Asn, Gly) alters the electrostatic environment, preventing drug binding.

Electrostatic reversal

e.g. F-N-S-K

Replacing Glu84 with Lys84 introduces a positive charge. This reverses the local electrostatic environment surrounding the Mg²⁺ ion, disrupting the coordination necessary for the drug-target complex despite maintaining ParC-80's hydrogen-bonding capacity.

Combined mean hydrophobicity

For each of GyrA and ParC, the mean hydrophobicity of the quinolone resistance-determining region helix is calculated on the Eisenberg consensus scale, and the combined value ⟨H⟩ GyrA-ParC is the average of the two. Helices carrying susceptible markers fall between 0.27 and 0.32, and helices carrying resistant markers between 0.40 and 0.48.
